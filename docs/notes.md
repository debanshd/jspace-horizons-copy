## the sentence

The reportable workspace holds a capacity-limited and modality-dependent subset of what a model takes in, and the model carries and uses items beyond that subset without any of them becoming reportable.

Every cell answers one consequence of it. B1: the subset has a capacity and the capacity depends on the modality. B2: the items beyond it still exist in the model (on the input tokens), and the capacity is or is not predicted by readout geometry. B3: the items beyond it are carried into later answers, and they sit inside or outside the span the lens reads. B4: the same on photographs. B5: the slope with size. B6: the frontier and the law, which is the scaling-laws-for-the-workspace idea made of B1 and B3's numbers. B7: whether the modality dependence is a dependence on words or on senses, with the audio channel in.

## the frontier and the law (B6)

Two quantities per modality per load, both measured: items said (in the lens at the report) and items used (carried into the answer with the scene gone). As k grows each modality traces a curve in the plane of said against used. The diagonal is a workspace that says what it uses. A modality's curve bending below the diagonal is the articulation bound as a shape. Each curve is fitted with a saturating form n(k) = K (1 - exp(-k / kappa)), separately for said and for used, so a modality has four numbers: a said-capacity, a used-capacity, and the load at which each saturates. The articulation ratio K_said / K_used is the share of what the workspace uses that it can say, one number per modality per model. The law in k is the fitted form and its parameters by modality. The law across size is the ratio per modality against parameters, drawn from the 4B and the 27B, two points, called a slope. The Pareto reading: whether the modalities fall on one curve (modality only moves a model along a single trade-off) or on separate curves (a trade-off per modality), decided by whether the picture's curve sits outside the text curve's interval at matched said. B6 needs no forwards and draws from the saved results, so it costs nothing to add and it is the figure that opens the talk.

## the claims, typed

Scientific: the number of present items the J-lens reads at the report position rises with load and stops, and the plateau is lower for a picture than for a list of the same items. Mechanistic: the state at the question positions, patched into a frame with no scene, carries items into a later answer that the lens did not read, and the carried content sits inside or outside the lens's span (decided by the part split with a dimension-matched random control). Methodological: the lens's count is validated against its own null (absent items under the same rule), the logit lens, a threshold sweep and a per-item AUROC, and the lens rank predicts carry per item against a permutation null. Cognitive analogy: the task instantiates a capacity-limited report over a wider store, the item-limit and report-limit distinction of working-memory work, as a functional analogy only. Safety: none claimed. Consciousness: none claimed. Architecture: Qwen3.5-4B and Qwen3.6-27B, no claim beyond them.

## the seven cells

B1, the bound. 12 shapes, 8 colours, k in {1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8}, 24 scenes per k, the same 24 scene specifications rendered as an English list, a Chinese list (English readout) and coloured icons on one 448 by 224 card (98 vision tokens at every k). One question in every rendering: "Which shapes are present? Answer with the shape names in English, separated by commas." At the report position (the last prompt token, the one the first answer token is predicted from), the J-lens rank of all 12 shape words at every read layer. A present shape is in J-space when its best rank is at or under 10 (the WorkspaceBench readout). Absent shapes under the same rule are the null. The logit lens is read alongside. Use: a fixed assistant turn ("Okay.") and a second user turn, "Is there a {shape}? Answer yes or no.", for four present shapes drawn at random from the scene and four absent, on the full scene. Capacity K is the largest mean count over k with a scene bootstrap, the knee the smallest k with mean at or above 0.9 K. Output: figB1_bound.

B2, the collapse. (a) The J-lens at every vision token of the card, for each present shape: readable on its own patches, anywhere on the card, at the report, by k, with absent shapes readable somewhere as the null. (b) The additive ceiling: the report state of each k = 1 scene minus a blank card's state gives one vector per shape, the k vectors of every k-scene are summed onto the blank state and read by the same lens, m_add(k) against m(k), text and picture. Output: figB2_collapse.

B3, carried. The scene replaced by a blank frame of the same rendering (a blank card, a list of "an item"), the model's state at the receipt positions (the question tokens and the report position, every layer) patched in from the real scene, the second turn asked from that state. Arms: full scene, blank frame alone, receipt at every layer, receipt at the report position alone, and at the read layers for k in {4, 8} on text and picture: the receipt's lens-readable part (top-256 right singular directions of W_U J_L), a random 256-dimensional part, the complement. Per present item: read (B1) by carried (p(yes) at or above 0.5 in the receipt arm) into four shares. Lens rank predicts carry as an AUROC with a permutation null. Output: figB3_carried.

B4, photographs. COCO val2017 under the POPE protocol, 20 images per k for k in {1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8} distinct single-token categories, letterboxed to 448 by 320 on grey. "Which objects are in the picture?", the same readout, the same null, POPE's random, popular and adversarial negatives, the receipt into a grey frame. Downloads val2017 (about 1 GB) and the annotations (about 240 MB) into B4_DIR, or point B4_DIR at an existing copy. Output: figB4_photographs.

B5, the slope. The per-model summary and the plot across models. Output: figB5_slope.

B6, the frontier. Said against used per modality with the diagonal, the fitted plateaus and knees, the articulation ratio per modality per model. Output: figB6_frontier.

B7, omni, on Gemma 4 E4B. The Neuronpedia lens repository holds fitted lenses for gemma-4-e2b, gemma-4-e4b and gemma-4-31b next to qwen3.5-0.8b, 2b, 4b, 9b, 27b and qwen3.6-27b ([neuronpedia/jacobian-lens](https://huggingface.co/neuronpedia/jacobian-lens/tree/main)), and the E2B and E4B take audio as well as image and video ([NVIDIA's model page for Gemma 4](https://docs.api.nvidia.com/nim/reference/google-gemma-4-31b-it)). So the audio arm runs today on a model with a lens, with no new instrument. Sixteen things that have a word, an emoji and a sound (dog, cat, cow, pig, sheep, frog, rooster, rain, baby, train, helicopter, siren, bell, airplane, fireworks, clock). A scene of k of them is rendered five ways: an English list (text), the list read aloud by espeak-ng (spoken), emoji on one card (picture), one emoji per frame (video), and the k ESC-50 sounds in sequence with 0.3 s gaps (sound). Two of the channels have a lexical and a non-lexical rendering, so the cell decides whether the articulation bound follows words or follows senses, which is the question the icon cells cannot ask. Measures as in B1 and B3: items in the lens at the report, use, items carried from the receipt into a blank frame (a blank card, blank frames, silence, a list of "an item"). Rules: the bound follows words when the silent share at k = 8 has a 95% lower bound at or above 0.10 for picture and sound and an upper bound under 0.10 for text and spoken. The channel matters when sound and picture differ by 0.15 with intervals apart. Both can fail, both can hold. Perception gate for the natural channels at hits 0.9 and false alarms 0.15 at k = 1, the carried control at 0.7. Dependencies: pip soundfile, apt espeak-ng (the spoken rendering is skipped without it, and the cell says so), the Noto Color Emoji font and ESC-50 (about 600 MB), both fetched by the cell. The cell encodes media through the model's own processor and builds the second turn from the template's own pieces, with a token-prefix assertion, so it is the same test on Qwen3.5 (minus audio) if the processor is swapped. Output: figB7_omni.

## preregistered rules, as coded

Perception (B1): a rendering is seen when hits at k = 1 are at or above 0.95 and false alarms at or below 0.10. A rendering that fails is drawn dotted and marked not seen, its lens numbers are not read. At each k the lens number is read where hits are at or above 0.9 (printed as the hold table). Photographs (B4): hits at or above 0.85 and false alarms at or below 0.20 at k = 1.

Bound by modality (B1): K_text minus K_picture with the 95% scene-bootstrap interval above 1. Amodal: the interval inside [-1, 1], which is an equivalence region and never a failed rejection. Copying: K_zh within 1 of K_picture and K_text above both by 2. No copying: K_zh within 1 of K_text. For the Chinese list a shape counts as held when either its English or its Chinese name is in the top-10, so a readout-language artefact cannot lower its count, and the English-only count is saved beside it. Every lens count is read under a second wording of the question too (B_ASK_ALT), and a plateau that moves by more than one item between wordings is reported as wording-dependent.

Serial position (B1): at k = 8 the share of present shapes read at the report by their place in the list, and by reading order on the card, is saved and printed. A primacy or recency shape is reported as found, with no rule attached, since no prediction was made.

Where (B2a): a gathering limit when present shapes readable anywhere on the card at k = 8 exceed the share readable at the report by 0.25 or more, with absent shapes readable somewhere under 0.3. An entry limit when the difference is under 0.1.

Additive (B2b): geometric when |m(k) - m_add(k)| is at or under 0.5 at every k. The model drops items when m(8) is below m_add(8) by 1 or more. A code when above by 1 or more.

Machinery (B3, B4): the k = 1 receipt carries its item on at least 0.8 of scenes (0.7 for photographs) and the blank frame alone has |d'| under 0.3. A failed control is printed first and the cell's verdict reads uninterpretable.

Bound (B3, B4): the silent share (carried and unread among present items) at k = 8 with a 95% lower bound at or above 0.10. Two versions: unread at the report position, and unread at every one of the patched receipt positions (the lens at all of them, every read layer). The verdict reads "a bound" only on the second, "a bound at the report only" when the word sits at an earlier receipt position, and the two numbers are printed side by side.

Where the carried content sits (B3): outside the lens's span when the complement arm's d' is at least 0.2 above both the lens-part arm's and the random part's. Inside when the lens part leads both by 0.2. Otherwise in both parts or in neither alone.

Layer: every read layer, best rank taken, the bench's rule. No layer picked on the data. The null is computed under the identical rule, so the multiplicity of taking the best over layers is in the null.

Position: the last prompt token (assistant prefix end), the position the first answer token is predicted from, asserted identical across renderings by token comparison.

Filters: none. Every scene is kept. The perception gate marks, it removes nothing.

## the hard-to-fake list, headline by headline, with the control that kills each

K_picture below K_text. The picture is not seen at high k: the perception gate and the hold table. The text count is copying from the context: the Chinese list. A threshold artefact: top-5, top-10, top-20 in the results and the per-item AUROC. Shape words that are not single tokens: printed at the top of B1 (read as the first token of the spaced form). The vision token count changes with k: it is 98 at every k. The lens is text-fit and reads pictures worse: the logit lens alongside, the k = 1 count as the positive control (Deb's 95 to 100% reproduced inside the design), and B2a reading every patch with the same lens. The model lists items in a fixed order and only the next one is at the report: the count is over all 12 shapes at once, and the shapes asked about are drawn at random so serial position never decides what is queried. The report position or the assistant prefix differs across renderings: asserted equal by token ids.

Words on the patches, missing at the report. The patch map assumes row-major 14 by 7 ordering: "anywhere on the card" is reported next to "on its own patches" and does not use the ordering. The patch readout reads absent shapes too: the absent-anywhere null.

Carried without a word. A yes bias in the receipt arm: d' and the false-alarm rate per arm, with the blank frame as the floor. The patch breaks the model: the k = 1 control, and the false-alarm rate in the receipt arm against the full scene's. The hook positions are wrong: token boundary assertions on the question start, the receipt tokens asserted identical between the scene prompt and the blank prompt, a shape assertion inside the hook. The lens misses by threshold alone: the lens-rank AUROC for carry with its permutation null, so a silent item is one the lens ranks low, and the whole rank distribution of carried against uncarried items is in the AUROC. The word sits at a question token instead of the report position: the silent share is recomputed with the lens at every patched position, and only that version earns the word bound. The Chinese list is low because the lens reads the Chinese token: the either-language count. One wording: the second wording's plateau. The part split is dimension-unfair: a random 256-dimensional part with the same hook. The "Okay." tokens leak scene content: they are recomputed in the blank frame from the patched state, the only channel.

Photographs. POPE yes bias: false alarms by negative type for the full scene, the receipt and the blank. Letterbox artefacts: the blank is the same grey frame. Multi-word categories: excluded, the single-word single-token vocabulary is saved in the results.

## the pilot, absorbed

Deb's pilot established, one object at a time, that the text-fit lens names a photo's object (95 to 100% against 12% chance), that swapping the concept flips the answer (85 to 100% against 0%), that the concept sits on the image tokens on the 27B (50 to 55% through the image against 5 to 10% through the question), and that the 4B says the object on 35% of items while the lens holds it on 90%. Those four are the k = 1 positive controls inside B1, B3 and B2 respectively, and the fourth is the articulation bound at k = 1, measured there without the carry test, so "says 35%, holds 90%" cannot yet separate silent carry from a re-read of the picture. B3 is what separates them. The three pilot ideas that did not hold each name a gate this program has: the printed word flipped with a second wording (the second wording is now run on every lens count), the hidden-hint AUROC of 0.52 had no power control and no load (every null here has its positive control and k is the dial), and the silent landmark step needed whole-word reads (the vocabulary here is single-token by construction and multi-token categories are excluded, with the list saved). A sweep across model families and forecasters answers nine questions once each. This program answers one question six ways, and the architecture claim stays at Qwen3.5 and Qwen3.6 until a second family runs the same cells.

## the omni dataset question

COCO under POPE is the right set for photographs alone: item-level ground truth, adversarial negatives, published Qwen numbers, and the objects have no word anywhere in the input. It is not omni, and the program's shape (one vocabulary, one load dial, one readout) wants a set where the same items arrive as text, as image, as video and as audio.

The open sets that are inherently omni are all built on YouTube ids, so a yt-dlp step is unavoidable for any of them. The structural match is MUSIC-AVQA ([Li et al. (2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14072)): 9,288 videos, 22 instruments, 45,867 questions with existential and counting questions asked of the audio, the video, and both, so the same instrument arrives through sound, through sight, and through its name, with the answer vocabulary already an item list. Its loads are small (71.7% of real videos are duets, 14.8% solos, 13.5% larger ensembles, with synthetic stitched videos reaching higher counts), so it gives the modality comparison at k of 1 to about 4 and the shape generator keeps the capacity curve. The wider set is VGGSound (sound classes with the video of the same event) and, for exact load control, ESC-50 clips concatenated into k-event scenes with the matching VGGSound frames: a generator in natural media, the same design as the shapes. OmniBench and AV-Odyssey are the omni benches people cite, and they are reasoning benches with no item-level ground truth, so they fail the benchmark gate (a set is used because it isolates the question).

What runs today, after the lens check above: B7 on Gemma 4 E4B, the generator in natural media (ESC-50 sounds, emoji, espeak), with audio in. MUSIC-AVQA is then the natural omni replication of B7 (the same instrument through sound, sight and name) once clips are on disk, and the natural video arm of B4's kind.

## what to say in sixty seconds

Show the model k things. Count how many of their names the lens reads at the report before it answers: the count stops at K, and K is lower for a picture than for a list of the same things, with the Chinese list saying whether the list's count is just the context. Take the scene away, keep only the model's state at the question, and ask: it answers about more things than K. The difference is what it uses and cannot say. Plot said against used as the load grows: each modality draws its own curve, the diagonal is a workspace that says what it uses, and the ratio of the plateaus is the share of its own cognition a model can put into words, per modality. Here is where the unsaid items are (on the patches, in the complement of what the lens reads), here is the same curve on photographs, here is the ratio across five sizes of one family, and here, on a model that hears, is whether the bound follows words or senses.
